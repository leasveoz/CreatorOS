CreatorOS 🌍

The Open-Source Ecosystem for Every Creator and Business

One platform. Every creator. Every business. Unlimited possibilities.

CreatorOS is an ambitious open-source initiative aiming to build a unified ecosystem of tools for content creators, influencers, agencies, entrepreneurs, e-commerce businesses and companies of all sizes.

Our mission is to make powerful digital business tools accessible to everyone, through free, open-source, secure and modular software.

🚀 Our Vision

We want to simplify the way people create content, manage their online presence, grow their businesses and automate their daily operations.

🧩 Planned Modules

* Creator Management: Tools for influencers, independent creators and subscription-based creators.
* Social Media Management: Centralized management of social media accounts, content and publishing.
* CRM: Customer relationship management, lead tracking and sales pipelines.
* Marketing & Advertising: Campaign management, analytics and marketing automation.
* E-commerce: Tools for online stores, brands and digital businesses.
* Agency Management: Client management, reporting, workflows and team collaboration.
* Automation: Reusable workflows, integrations and productivity tools.
* Developer Ecosystem: APIs, SDKs, plugins and community-built extensions.

🔐 Security & Privacy

Security, privacy and responsible development are fundamental principles.

We aim to use official platform APIs, follow their policies, protect user data and build reliable software.

🌐 Open Source & Community

CreatorOS is intended to be community-driven.

We want developers worldwide to participate, contribute code, build integrations, report issues and help expand the ecosystem.

🛠️ Development Status

Early planning and development stage.

The architecture, initial modules and development roadmap are being prepared. Contributions and community participation will be welcomed as the project evolves.

🎯 Long-Term Goal

To build a comprehensive, accessible and sustainable open-source infrastructure that helps creators, developers and businesses succeed in the digital world.
